What Is Photography?

Photography is the art of capturing light to freeze a moment in time. A single frame can show a place, a mood, or a small detail most people walk right past.

  • Shoot landscapes, buildings, and street scenes.
  • Chase good light at sunrise and sunset.
  • Look for interesting angles and patterns.

Who I Am

I am a student who loves taking photos around campus and wherever I travel. I love capturing photos with my phone and recently upgraded to a DSLR camera, which I use sometimes.

I like all types of photos, but especially bright sunset or sunrise photos for their color.

  • I enjoy taking photos around campus.
  • I like experimenting with phone and DSLR cameras.
  • I especially enjoy colorful sunrises and sunsets.

Best Times to Shoot

Light changes everything in photography. The best photos often happen when sunlight is soft and colorful, but any time can be a good time to take pictures.

Light by time of day
Time Light Best For
Sunrise Soft and warm Landscapes
Midday Harsh and bright Architecture
Golden hour Golden and low Portraits and buildings
Blue hour Cool and deep City scenes and lamps

Best Places to Shoot

You do not need to travel far to take good pictures. Interesting photo spots are often nearby if you look carefully and notice what catches your eye.

  • Campus walkways: Brick paths, lamps, and tall buildings.
  • The beach: Reflections and glowing sunsets.
  • City streets: Strong lines, signs, and moody skies.

How to Get Started

  1. Pick a camera or a phone. Either is perfectly fine to begin with.
  2. Learn aperture, shutter speed, and ISO.
  3. Practice composition, such as leading lines and the rule of thirds.
  4. Edit lightly if needed and share the best shots.

My favorite tip: change your angle. Getting low or looking up can turn a plain wall into a strong picture.

  • Try different angles before taking a photo.
  • Pay attention to lighting and shadows.
  • Keep practicing with your phone or camera.

Why I Love It

Photography makes me slow down and notice details I would normally miss. It gives me a chance to express myself and capture moments that make me happy. Even a walk back to my dorm from classes, a camera in hand, helps me see the beauty in ordinary things and brightens my day.

It also lets me save memories and share how a place felt to me, not just how it looked.

  • Photography helps me notice small details.
  • It lets me save meaningful memories.
  • It gives me a creative way to express myself.
